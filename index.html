The complete decision

A model can recommend.
Only a person can be accountable.

Most review tools capture a label. Docket captures the decision itself — who made it, what they saw, which rule applied, where reviewers disagreed, who resolved it, and whether the record was later changed.

  • 01
    Who made it A named human reviewer is attached to every ruling. AI cannot sign.
  • 02
    What evidence they reviewed Policies, representative conversations, prior rulings, and the packet prepared for them.
  • 03
    Which policy or rule was applied Versioned, deterministic rules — not a black-box similarity score.
  • 04
    Where reviewers disagreed Independent opinions stay on the record instead of being overwritten.
  • 05
    Who resolved the disagreement Adjudication is a separate, signed act — not a silent edit.
  • 06
    Whether the record was changed later Corrections append. The original judgment remains readable.

How it works

From thousands of conversations
to one defensible decision.

  1. 1

    Group by what went wrong

    Versioned rules organize conversations into reproducible issue groups — inaccurate coverage, missing disclosures, unsafe advice, privacy violations, and the failures that matter to your policies.

  2. 2

    Review the evidence

    Human reviewers inspect representative examples, relevant policies, previous rulings, and AI-prepared evidence before anything is committed.

  3. 3

    Prove every ruling

    Decisions, disagreement, corrections, reviewer identities, and rule versions enter a tamper-evident audit trail that an organization or auditor can verify.

Human authorization

AI prepares the review.
Humans make the decision.

Docket uses AI to make review faster, not to take responsibility. A named reviewer always remains accountable.

AI assistance

What AI is allowed to do

  • Summarize evidence
  • Find similar previous rulings
  • Explain which rule was triggered
  • Propose classification rules
  • Draft a rationale
  • Assemble review packets and reports
Human authorization

What AI cannot do

  • Submit a verdict
  • Propagate a decision
  • Hide reviewer disagreement
  • Replace human approval
  • Change previous records
